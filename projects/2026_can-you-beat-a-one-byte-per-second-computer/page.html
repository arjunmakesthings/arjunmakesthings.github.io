can you beat a 1-byte-per-second computer?

2026

interactive-installation,
at nyu-itp.

in a world where 'intelligence' is highly-debated, human-beings are increasingly faced with absurd questions — am i smarter than a machine? and, if so, for how long? by how much? and, in what all? to me & aram pundak, this formed a compelling premise for a showdown.

we built an installation for a human to go head-to-head with a barebones machine — capable of thinking only in 1-byte-per-second — in a game of wordle. the machine was programmed to learn via a genetic-algorithm, and gets closer with every guess entered into the system. wrong guesses — if far off from the answer — are punished by the host with a big, red (foam) slapping hand.

i worked on the programming & electronics, while aram handled the fabrication, and we exhibited this at the itp-spring-show in 2026.

the making is extensively documented here, while the code + build-details are available on github.
